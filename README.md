The cup is not a cylinder, so the volume does not grow evenly with height. Think of it as an upside-down cone whose tip (the shpitz) has been cut off.

Similar triangles give the full height of that cone from the cup's height and its two radii. The volume of a cone is V = ⅓πr2h, so subtracting the missing tip from the full cone gives the cup's volume, and solving the same formula for one reviis gives the fill height.
